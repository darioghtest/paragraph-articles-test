Taskline lets you write down what needs doing and check it off when it's done, without creating an account. It's a small task list from our team, and it's for anyone who wants to keep track of their to-dos without signing up for another service first.

## Add a task in one line

You type what needs doing and press Add, and it joins your list. There are no projects or extra fields to fill in first, so you can write a task down the moment you think of it.

## Check tasks off as you finish them

When you finish a task, tick it off. It stays on your list marked done, so you can see what you've already finished. If something needs another pass, untick it and it's back on your plate.

## Your list is saved in your browser

There's no password to remember. To keep working from the same list, open Taskline in the same browser on the same computer each time.

## How to start

Taskline runs from a single file on your own computer. Download it from the [Taskline repository on GitHub](https://github.com/darioghtest/taskline), open the index.html file in your browser, and write down the first thing on your list.
